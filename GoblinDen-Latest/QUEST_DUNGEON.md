# Sealed Descent — three-floor quest dungeon

This is an expandable dungeon prototype appended after the existing last area; the overall game layout remains a staging arrangement.

## Find and explore

In **Frostroot Cavern**, approach the eastern **Sealed Descent** marker and press **E**. Each of the three floors has a 2880 × 1920 play area, with connected stone chambers, corridors, side vaults and return stairs. A fog-of-exploration mini-map shows your position and only the ground and landmarks you have actually discovered. Unvisited chambers, stairs and ward seals stay hidden. The second floor mirrors the route to change the direction of exploration; the bottom floor opens into a larger final arena.

On floors 1 and 2, activate both ward seals before descending. They are in separate branches, so explore the side routes. Seals remain active after you leave or reload, and defeated dungeon guards remain cleared after a save. You can return upstairs and leave the dungeon at any time. Stored items and corpse recovery continue to use the existing rules. The floor camera follows across the full dungeon bounds.

The final floor contains a new surprise boss, followed by a sealed reward reliquary. Its identity and attack specifics are deliberately omitted here. After recovering the reliquary, return to **Rusk** at Bramble Market for the quest reward. Both the final cache and quest reward pay only once. The backpack menu includes a short quest-progress summary.

## Animation and encounter work

The boss has a dormant appearance, reveal sequence, idle and movement motion, three distinct attacks with locked ground/directional warnings, recovery openings, a low-health phase transition, hit recoil/flash, and an animated defeat with cleanup of lingering projectiles. It uses existing painted materials plus new native animation and effects. Room transitions cancel pending attacks; the boss cannot grant completion twice or respawn after a saved defeat.

The dungeon includes placed guards with increasing health/damage/XP across the floors and optional supply caches. Combat numbers and encounter pacing remain starting values for human playtesting.

## Verified

Passed in Godot 4.5.2: entry from the last existing area; all three floor bounds; navigable routes to wards, stairs and final chamber; collision-free marker positions; seal-gated descent; return stairs; actual actor movement through physical corridors; telegraph locking and slam avoidance; bounded projectile attack; second-phase activation; one-time boss/cache/quest rewards; and persistence across a full scene reload. Rendered all floor scenes and the boss's reveal, movement, attack, recovery, hit and defeat states. Existing RPG integration and den-doorway tests passed.

The physical-route test found a navigation/wall-edge mismatch. Navigation now uses the same occupied wall cells as collision, plus actor clearance, and the repeated traversal test passed.
