# Audio credits

The audio added in this update was composed and synthesized specifically for GoblinDen during this task. No recordings, sample packs, downloaded songs or third-party music libraries were used.

The score is original note-based ambient fantasy music with synthesized harp/plucked strings, woodwinds, soft string pads, bells and restrained percussion. Effects use original synthesized tones and noise envelopes. Source audio is included in assets/audio, and the generator is included in tools/compose_audio.py.

Track files: music_haven.wav, music_woodland.wav, music_descent.wav.

Effects: hit, hurt, swing, dodge, interact, bow, magic, loot, coin, potion, death, level_up, stairs, boss, step_stone and step_grass (WAV).
