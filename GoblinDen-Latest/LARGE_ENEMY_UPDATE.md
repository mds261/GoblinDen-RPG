# Expanded-room large enemies

Each of the three new areas now contains a unique miniboss encounter:

- **Rootvein Grotto — Rootvein Giant Spider:** fast pounces, quick recovery, 320 HP.
- **The Cave Mouth — Cave-Mouth Ogre:** slow movement, heavily telegraphed area slam, 480 HP.
- **Thornwood Hollow — Thornwood Wolf Rider:** very fast pursuit and repeated spear charges, 370 HP.

The Spider and Ogre must be defeated before the player can continue through the right-hand passage. All three creatures use visible attack zones, named boss health bars, resistance to full-duration stuns, large death effects, and increased scrap/XP rewards.
