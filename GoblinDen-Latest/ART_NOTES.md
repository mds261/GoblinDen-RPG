# Art generation notes

These are AI-generated images in a hand-painted dark-fantasy style, made with the built-in image-generation tool. No CLI/API fallback was used. Original outputs were copied into `assets/`; the game performs scaling, mirroring and atlas-region rendering without altering the source images. Transparent alpha was verified for the characters and prop atlas.

## Final prompt: assets/cave.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: actual background image for a top-down 2D action RPG. Create a wide landscape 16:10 hand-painted dark-fantasy goblin cave battle arena, camera nearly straight down with slight three-quarter view on perimeter rocks. Beautiful textured brushwork, weathered charcoal limestone, muted moss green, amber fire accents, ominous and earthy. Exact gameplay composition: a rectangular open floor occupies x=7% to93%, y=14% to86% of image. The ENTIRE central area must be walkable flat worn stone, with only tiny flat pebbles, subtle cracks, faint old ritual circle at center. All tall rocks, cave walls, mushrooms, torches and scenery must be confined to outermost 7% on left/right and outermost 14% at top/bottom. Do NOT place rocks, structures, raised obstacles or props in central walkable area. No foreground occluders over floor. Dim torch sconces at left and right perimeter, tiny teal fungi only on outer edges. Strongly readable central floor, atmospheric shadows at border, subtle warm lighting. No characters, no monsters, no weapons, no UI, no text, no logos, no treasure. Finished painterly game environment, not pixel art, not cartoon outlines, not photorealistic 3D. Rectangular room fills frame with no margins or frame.
