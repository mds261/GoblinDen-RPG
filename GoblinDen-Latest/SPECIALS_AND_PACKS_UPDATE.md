# Signature specials and monster packs

## Player specials

- **Blade — Shadow Flurry:** longer invulnerable dash with five animated teal slash trails and shadow afterimages.
- **Archer — Venom Starfall:** rotating poison rune, upgraded snare, and a five-arrow venom barrage.
- **Knight — Ironquake Bulwark:** large shield crest, concentric shockwaves, ground cracks, stun, and an extended guard stance.
- **Mage — Astral Hex Nova:** layered purple-blue rune explosion with orbiting spell motes, poison, curse, and slow.

All four specials now have class-colored screen flashes, stronger camera impact, and larger readable effects.

## Large-enemy signatures

- The **Giant Spider** summons up to four spiderlings and periodically places persistent poison clouds that damage goblins standing inside.
- The **Cave-Mouth Ogre** periodically charges a 1.75-second Cavebreaker smash with a much larger danger circle, increased damage, shockwave, and ground-crack art.
- The **Wolf Rider** begins with three Dire Wolves and replenishes fallen pack members while the rider lives.

## Single-goblin opening

The save version has been advanced so this build starts clean with one playable Goblin and no recruited companions. Pressing **R** now creates a genuinely fresh single-Goblin run; ordinary progression remains protected through the existing autosaves.
