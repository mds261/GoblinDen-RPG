# Expanded world update

The original Polished v1 route now continues beyond **The Breach** through three new painted areas:

1. **Rootvein Grotto** — an old root-bound chamber with glowcaps and an underground stream.
2. **The Cave Mouth** — the goblin den's guarded entrance, lit by daylight from the forest.
3. **Thornwood Hollow** — a hidden exterior clearing with lookouts, thorn wards, and misty woodland.

## How to reach the new rooms

Clear the human defenders in Ashen Passage and The Breach. After the Iron Captain falls, continue right through the newly opened route. Use **E** at each marked passage. The final exterior room has no artificial onward gate; use the left passage to return to the cave.
